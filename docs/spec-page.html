What was settled since v1

  • DECIDEDCamera tier locked at 5MP AF. Downgrading to 2MP/fixed-focus makes this a worse version of a buyable product, and the toy keyring cameras that work as gifts all live at this quality tier. An MCU (ESP32-C3) cost-down saves only ~AU$3 and forfeits autofocus permanently — rejected. So is the MCU-less IP-cam-SoC route (Ingenic-class): proprietary SDK, no AF, research project not a product.
  • DECIDEDGeneric 24P DVP camera map. PCB is camera-agnostic at order time: modules are hand-attached FPC parts, bought whenever budget allows. The generic ESP32-CAM map opens the whole cheap module tier and excludes only Seeed's XIAO-locked module (~AU$23). Pin 24 carries AF power — the one pin to route right.
  • −AU$15Camera pricing verified down. 78mm AF OV5640 24P DVP modules on AliExpress: AU$10.91–12.49 from 4.8–4.9★ sellers with 115–374 sales, free shipping, ~Dec 4 arrival if ordered by ~Nov 25. Wildcard: one listing sells an AF-variant at AU$1.64 — 3–4 teardown samples (~AU$10) settle whether it's a real VCM lens and collapses the AF tier.
  • −AU$3.30Battery upgraded and cheapened. Ecocell (Castle Hill NSW) 402025 150mAh with protection board and JST plug: AU$5.03+GST at 10x, 273 in stock — replaces the bare 401230 (protection circuitry moves onto the cell, no soldered leads, +25% capacity).
  • correctedLilyGo's US$2.80 was the 2MP OV2640. The 5MP FF OV5640 is US$11.90 (~AU$18) — the "reputable fixed-focus at $2.80" anchor was wrong and is now retired. Reputable FF still exists at ~AU$18 if you ever want it as plan-B.
  • KEPTMCU stays ESP32-S3FH4R2. Re-verified: every Espressif S3 module is Economic-Assembly-incompatible at JLCPCB (forces Standard PCBA at $25–51 setup) — the certified-module safety route costs more and doesn't fit the board. In-package 4MB flash + 2MB PSRAM confirmed against Espressif's datasheet.
  • BUILTCase exists. Two parametric variants (BMO-face with 4-key game keypad; retro mini-camera) — validated STL/STEP in ~/keyring-camera/case/, no supports, snap-fit, keyring post for a standard 6mm split ring. One script, all dims editable when samples arrive.

PCB design spec — deltas that matter

Outline & panel

  • ~25 × 26mm, 2-layer, 0.8mm; r ≈ 3mm corner notch under the keyring post lug (post stands 1–2.7mm proud of the outline).
  • Order as single PCBs, NOT a panel — live quote verified: a 4-across panel adds ~$37 in per-panel minimum charges to avoid a ~$5 small-board surcharge. Panelizing v1 advice reversed by the real fee table.
  • OSP finish (ENIG for money is gone since July 2026).

RF

  • Bare chip → meandered inverted-F antenna, top edge, Espressif keep-out. HexSense's 17.5×30.5mm board is the proportions reference.
  • No copper/parts in the antenna zone; battery bay and SD below the ground pour.

Edge placement (case-driven)

  • USB-C top edge centred (case: 9.4mm mouth at x −0.5).
  • TF slot right edge (12 × 3.4mm mouth). Camera FPC top-left with the Ø6.4 lens aperture; display FPC behind its glass; shutter button left wall at y 10.4, function right wall at y 10.9.
  • Battery bay is the case's full-length rear strip ( Ecocell 402025 on JST leads, glued); speaker fires down through the lid grille at its case bay.

Behavior shipped in firmware

  • Deep sleep ~15µA → ~260 days idle; double-press wakes straight into find-me (chirp + WiFi SoftAP).
  • 30-min "am I home" scan (~75µA avg) → ~2 months between charges and a last-seen timestamp.
  • AF via esp32-camera PR #831 (ov5640_af); PDM mic + MAX98357 shutter/find-me audio; games on the 4-key pad (+2 side buttons).

Geometry & print

  • 28 × 44.2mm body × 12.4mm — the 40mm-class battery cannot fit under a 25mm PCB; the elongation is what gives the keypad + speaker bays room.
  • Snap-fit hooks + lid, all STLs validated watertight; no supports anywhere; 0.16mm layers (0.12 for the gift run), 3–4 walls, ironing on the face.
  • Re-tune when samples arrive: display glass proud-height, FPC fold gap, barrel-vs-ring, snap clearance — all top-of-script variables.

Ruled out — do not re-litigate

  • deadMCU-less / IP-cam-SoC (Ingenic-class): proprietary SDK, no AF driver, months of firmware risk. The proprietary-wall the builder already suspected is real.
  • deadRaw OV5640 die + standalone AF barrel: die out of stock at LCSC (~$10+ brokering), barrels US$10–14 — 3–5× a finished module.
  • deadEspressif S3 modules (WROOM-1, MINI-1): verified Economic-Assembly-incompatible at JLCPCB; MINI-1 also collides with the FPC zones.
  • deadESP32-C3 cost-down: saves ~AU$3, forfeits PSRAM → forfeits 5MP and AF. Kept S3FH4R2.
  • deadLIR coin cells, bare TFT glass, always-on BLE beacons, OV3660 (no AF support in any driver), 2MP downgrade (worse version of a buyable).
